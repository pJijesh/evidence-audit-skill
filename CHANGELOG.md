## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-11

### Added

- The evidence-audit skill: a six-phase process, three statuses, and eight attack-class guides.
- Scripts that verify quoted evidence against the audited files, validate a coverage ledger, and build a deterministic report.
- JSON Schemas for findings and the coverage ledger.
- A worked audit of a deliberately vulnerable fictional app, with its findings, ledger and report.
- Installers for Claude Code, Codex, OpenCode and Cursor, with generated adapters.
- Community files, citation metadata and a Dependabot configuration.
